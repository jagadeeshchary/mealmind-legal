MealMinder Account Deletion

This page explains how you can request deletion of your MealMinder account and associated data.

Delete Your Account From the MealMinder App

If you still have access to MealMinder, you can delete your account directly from the app.

Open MealMinder, go to your Profile section, select the account deletion option, and follow the confirmation steps.

Important: Account deletion is permanent. Once deletion is completed, the account and associated data cannot be restored through MealMinder.

Request Deletion Without the App

If you no longer have access to MealMinder, you may request account deletion by contacting:

whatnotapps@gmail.com

Please use the subject:

MealMinder Account Deletion Request

In your request, provide the email address associated with your MealMinder account so that we can identify the account.

We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the deletion request.

Do not send passwords, authentication codes, or other sensitive credentials by email.

Data That Is Deleted

When your account deletion request is successfully processed, MealMinder is designed to delete data associated with your account, including where applicable:

  • your MealMinder authentication account;
  • meal and calorie logs associated with your account;
  • nutrition records associated with your account;
  • profile image data stored for your MealMinder account;
  • other cloud data directly associated with your MealMinder account.

Local Device Data

MealMinder also stores some information locally on your device.

Local MealMinder data is removed by the app as part of the account-deletion process where applicable.

You may also remove local application data by uninstalling MealMinder or clearing the application's storage through Android system settings.

Data That May Be Retained

We may retain limited information where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or regulatory purposes.

Any retained information will only be kept for as long as reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose.

Processing Time

We will process valid deletion requests within a reasonable period after successfully verifying account ownership.
