MealMind Privacy Policy\

\

Last updated: November 17, 2026 \

\

This Privacy Policy explains how MealMind ("MealMind", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the MealMind Android application. \

\

By using MealMind, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. \

\

1. Information We Collect\

\

MealMind may collect or process the following types of information: \

\

Account Information\

\

When you create or sign in to an account, authentication information may be processed through Firebase Authentication. \

\

If you choose Google Sign-In, Google may provide basic account information such as your name, email address, and account identifier. \

\

Meal and Nutrition Information\

\

MealMind allows you to record meals, calories, macronutrients, nutrition values, dates, and related food-tracking information. \

\

This information may be stored locally on your device and, when you are signed in, may also be synchronized with our cloud database. \

\

Profile Image\

\

If you choose to add a profile image, MealMind may process an image selected from your device or captured using your camera. \

\

The image may be compressed and stored as profile data associated with your account. \

\

Device Permissions\

\

MealMind may request access to your camera when you choose to take a profile photo. \

\

MealMind may also request notification permission so that it can provide reminders or app notifications. \

\

These permissions are only used when required for the related feature. \

\

Food Search Requests\

\

When you search for food information, MealMind may send your search query to our backend service so that nutrition information can be retrieved from third-party food databases. \

\

\

We use information to: \

\
    \
  • provide account authentication and sign-in functionality;\
    • \
  • save and synchronize meal and nutrition records;\
    • \
  • display calorie and nutrition information;\
    • \
  • support food search functionality;\
    • \
  • display your selected profile image;\
    • \
  • provide reminders and notifications when enabled;\
    • \
  • maintain, secure, and improve the application;\
    • \
  • support account deletion and data-management requests.\

4. Local Device Storage\

\

MealMind stores some information locally on your device using Android storage technologies such as Room database and DataStore. \

\

Local information may include meal records, food information, preferences, and application state required for features such as offline usage. \

\

5. Cloud Storage\

\

When you are signed in, MealMind may synchronize certain account-related data with cloud services. \

\

This may include: \

\
    \
  • meal and calorie logs;\
    • \
  • nutrition information associated with saved meals;\
    • \
  • profile image data;\
    • \
  • basic account-related information.\
    • \
\

6. Data Sharing\

\

MealMind does not sell your personal information. \

\

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the application, such as authentication, cloud storage, backend infrastructure, and nutrition-data providers. \

\

We may also disclose information if required by applicable law or a valid legal process. \

\

7. Data Security\

\

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect user information. \

\

MealMind uses authenticated access controls for account-related cloud data and routes external nutrition API access through a backend service so private API credentials are not stored directly in the Android application. \

\

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. \

\

8. Data Retention\

\

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide MealMind services, maintain your account, satisfy legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements. \

\

When an account is deleted, MealMind is designed to remove associated cloud account data and locally stored MealMind data, subject to any information we may be legally required to retain. \

\

9. Account and Data Deletion\

\

You can request deletion of your MealMind account and associated data. \

\

If you have access to the app, you may use the account deletion option available in MealMind. \

\

If you no longer have access to the app, you may request account deletion using our external account deletion page: \

\

\ MealMind Account Deletion \ \

10. Children's Privacy\

\

MealMind is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. \

\

If you believe a child has provided personal information through MealMind, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action. \

\

11. Health and Nutrition Information\

\

MealMind provides food, calorie, and nutrition-tracking features for general informational and wellness purposes. \

\

MealMind is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional healthcare advice. \

\

Nutrition information may come from third-party databases and may contain inaccuracies or variations. \

\

You should consult an appropriate healthcare professional for medical or dietary decisions that require professional advice. \

\
