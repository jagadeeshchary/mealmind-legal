Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MealMind (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) collects, uses, and protects user information.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Name (if provided)
  • Email address
  • Authentication credentials via secure providers

User-Generated Data

  • Food entries typed by the user
  • Quantity in grams (optional)
  • Selected health goal
  • Daily meal completion status
  • Monthly tracking history

Technical Information

  • Device type
  • App usage events
  • Crash logs
  • Performance diagnostics

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:
  • Provide food logging functionality
  • Store and display daily meal status
  • Generate monthly statistics and pattern insights
  • Provide motivational content based on user goals
  • Maintain account authentication
  • Improve app performance and stability

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data.

3. Data Storage and Security

User data is stored using secure cloud infrastructure, including authentication services and cloud databases. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are implemented to protect data from unauthorized access.

4. Third-Party Services

MealMind uses trusted third-party infrastructure such as:
  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Cloud Storage
These services process only the information necessary to operate the application.

5. Notifications

If reminders are enabled, the app may schedule local notifications to encourage meal logging. Notification settings can be managed within the app or device settings.

6. Account Deletion

Users may delete their account directly within the app via the Profile section. Upon deletion, associated account data is removed from active systems in accordance with technical processes.

7. Children’s Privacy

MealMind is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the app after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
