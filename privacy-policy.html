MealMinder Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MealMinder ("MealMinder", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the MealMinder Android application.

By using MealMinder, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

MealMinder may collect or process the following types of information:

Account Information

When you create or sign in to an account, authentication information may be processed through Firebase Authentication.

If you choose Google Sign-In, Google may provide basic account information such as your name, email address, and account identifier.

Meal and Nutrition Information

MealMinder allows you to record meals, calories, macronutrients, nutrition values, dates, and related food-tracking information.

This information may be stored locally on your device and, when you are signed in, may also be synchronized with our cloud database.

Profile Image

If you choose to add a profile image, MealMinder may process an image selected from your device or captured using your camera.

The image may be compressed and stored as profile data associated with your account.

Device Permissions

MealMinder may request access to your camera when you choose to take a profile photo.

MealMinder may also request notification permission so that it can provide reminders or app notifications.

These permissions are only used when required for the related feature.

Food Search Requests

When you search for food information, MealMinder may send your search query to our backend service so that nutrition information can be retrieved from third-party food databases.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • provide account authentication and sign-in functionality;
  • save and synchronize meal and nutrition records;
  • display calorie and nutrition information;
  • support food search functionality;
  • display your selected profile image;
  • provide reminders and notifications when enabled;
  • maintain, secure, and improve the application;
  • support account deletion and data-management requests.

3. Services We Use

MealMinder relies on third-party services to provide certain functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Services used by MealMinder may include:

  • Google Firebase – used for authentication and cloud data storage.
  • Google Sign-In – used when you choose to authenticate using your Google account.
  • Cloudflare – used as a backend service for securely processing food-search requests.
  • USDA FoodData Central – used to retrieve food and nutrition information.

4. Local Device Storage

MealMinder stores some information locally on your device using Android storage technologies such as Room database and DataStore.

Local information may include meal records, food information, preferences, and application state required for features such as offline usage.

5. Cloud Storage

When you are signed in, MealMinder may synchronize certain account-related data with cloud services.

This may include:

  • meal and calorie logs;
  • nutrition information associated with saved meals;
  • profile image data;
  • basic account-related information.

6. Data Sharing

MealMinder does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the application, such as authentication, cloud storage, backend infrastructure, and nutrition-data providers.

We may also disclose information if required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect user information.

MealMinder uses authenticated access controls for account-related cloud data and routes external nutrition API access through a backend service so private API credentials are not stored directly in the Android application.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide MealMinder services, maintain your account, satisfy legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements.

When an account is deleted, MealMinder is designed to remove associated cloud account data and locally stored MealMinder data, subject to any information we may be legally required to retain.

9. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your MealMinder account and associated data.

If you have access to the app, you may use the account deletion option available in MealMinder.

If you no longer have access to the app, you may request account deletion using our external account deletion page:

MealMinder Account Deletion

10. Children's Privacy

MealMinder is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through MealMinder, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

11. Health and Nutrition Information

MealMinder provides food, calorie, and nutrition-tracking features for general informational and wellness purposes.

MealMinder is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional healthcare advice.

Nutrition information may come from third-party databases and may contain inaccuracies or variations.

You should consult an appropriate healthcare professional for medical or dietary decisions that require professional advice.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date shown at the top of this page.
