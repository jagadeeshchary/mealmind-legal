Frequently Asked Questions

How does MealMind calculate calories?

MealMind uses nutrition databases and AI estimation to provide approximate values based on the food you type. The numbers are meant for awareness, not exact measurement.

Why are my calories slightly different from other apps?

Different apps use different databases and serving assumptions. Small differences are normal.

Do I need to enter exact grams?

No. Grams improve accuracy but are optional.

Why does the app show patterns instead of strict targets?

MealMind focuses on understanding habits rather than enforcing dieting rules.

Can I track homemade food?

Yes. Just describe the dish and the app estimates nutrition based on common recipes.

Is this medical advice?

No. MealMind is an awareness tool, not a medical program.

Will my data be shared?

No. Your food logs remain private.

The app is not working properly

Try:

  1. Restart the app
  2. Check your internet connection
  3. Update to the latest version
