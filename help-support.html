MealMind Help & Support

How does MealMind calculate nutrition values?

MealMind uses nutrition information stored in the app and information retrieved from external nutrition databases.

Nutrition values are scaled according to the quantity or serving selected when logging a food.

Why can nutrition values differ from other apps?

Nutrition values may differ between databases because of differences in food preparation, brands, serving sizes, recipes, and data sources.

MealMind provides nutrition values for general tracking and awareness purposes.

How do serving sizes work?

Some foods are tracked by weight, such as grams, while certain foods may support piece-based servings.

MealMind uses the available serving information to estimate the nutrition values for the quantity you select.

Can I use MealMind without an internet connection?

MealMind supports local meal storage so you can continue recording eligible meal information when your internet connection is unavailable.

When connectivity returns, pending meal information may be synchronized with your MealMind account.

Why is a food search not returning results?

Some food searches require an internet connection to retrieve additional nutrition information.

Try checking your internet connection, using a simpler food name, or searching again later.

How do I change my profile image?

Open your Profile section and use the available profile image option.

Depending on your selection, MealMind may request permission to use your camera or allow you to choose an image from your device.

Why am I not receiving reminders?

Check that notifications are enabled for MealMind in your Android device settings.

On supported Android versions, MealMind requires notification permission before reminders can be displayed.

How do I delete my account?

If you have access to the app, open the Profile section and use the account deletion option.

If you cannot access the app, visit the MealMind Account Deletion page.

The app is not working properly

Try the following:

  1. close and reopen MealMind;
  2. check your internet connection;
  3. make sure you are using the latest available version;
  4. restart your device if the problem continues.
