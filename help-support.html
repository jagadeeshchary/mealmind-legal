Help & Support

How does MealMind calculate calories?

MealMind uses nutrition databases and estimation systems to provide approximate calorie values based on the food and quantity entered.

Why are calories slightly different from other apps?

Different platforms use different data sources and assumptions. Minor differences are normal.

Do I need to enter exact grams?

No. Grams improve accuracy but are optional. If not provided, 100g is assumed by default.

What meals are tracked?

The app groups entries into Breakfast, Lunch, Snacks, and Dinner to show daily completion status.

How are monthly stats calculated?

The system calculates days logged, average calories, tracking streaks, and most logged foods based on stored entries.

Why does the app show patterns?

Pattern detection highlights recurring behaviors within a selected month to help users understand habits.

The app is not working properly

  1. Restart the app
  2. Check your internet connection
  3. Update to the latest version
