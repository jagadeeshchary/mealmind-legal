About MealMinder

MealMinder is an Android application designed to help users understand and track their daily food intake through simple meal logging, calorie awareness, and nutrition information.

What MealMinder Does

MealMinder helps users record meals and review nutrition information in a simple and practical way.

Features may include:

  • searching for foods and nutrition information;
  • logging meals and calorie intake;
  • tracking protein, carbohydrates, fat, fiber, sugar, and sodium;
  • viewing daily and monthly food history;
  • using common Indian foods and nutrition data from external food databases;
  • saving meal history for offline access;
  • synchronizing meal data with a signed-in MealMinder account;
  • setting reminders and receiving notifications when enabled;
  • managing profile information and profile images.

Food and Nutrition Information

MealMinder uses both locally available food data and external nutrition databases to provide food and nutrition information.

Nutrition values may vary depending on food preparation, serving size, manufacturer, recipe, and data source.

Values displayed by MealMinder should therefore be treated as estimates for general awareness and tracking purposes.

Offline and Cloud Sync

MealMinder is designed to allow meal tracking even when an internet connection is temporarily unavailable.

When you are signed in and internet access becomes available, eligible meal records may be synchronized with MealMinder's cloud services.

Our Purpose

MealMinder is intended to make food tracking easier to understand and maintain. The goal is to support awareness of eating habits and nutrition without requiring unnecessarily complicated tracking workflows.

Health Disclaimer

MealMinder provides general nutrition and wellness information only.

MealMinder is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, dietary prescriptions, or professional healthcare advice.

Users should consult an appropriate healthcare professional for medical, nutritional, or dietary decisions that require professional guidance.
