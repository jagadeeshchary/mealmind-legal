About MealMind

MealMind is a mobile application designed to help users understand their daily food intake through simple food logging and calorie awareness.

Our Purpose

MealMind focuses on simplicity. Instead of complex calorie tracking systems, strict dieting plans, or overwhelming charts, the app provides a lightweight experience where users can:

  • Log food quickly using text input
  • View daily meal completion status
  • Track monthly consistency
  • Discover simple eating patterns

How It Works

Users type a food name and optional quantity in grams. If no quantity is provided, the app assumes 100 grams by default. The system estimates calorie values and stores the entry for daily and monthly insights.

The Home screen provides:

  • Today’s meal completion status (Breakfast, Lunch, Snacks, Dinner)
  • Monthly statistics (days logged, streaks, averages)
  • Pattern insights when applicable
  • Motivational messages based on user goals

Our Philosophy

MealMind promotes awareness, not restriction. The goal is clarity, not pressure. By highlighting patterns and trends, users can build sustainable habits over time.

Important Notice

MealMind provides general nutrition information for awareness purposes only. It is not a medical, dietary, or clinical service. Users should consult qualified healthcare professionals for medical or dietary advice.
