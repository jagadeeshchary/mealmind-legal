About MealMind

What is MealMind?

MealMind helps you understand what you eat — not judge it.

Instead of strict calorie counting, MealMind focuses on awareness. Just type your food and see simple nutrition insights instantly.

Over time, the app highlights patterns in your eating habits so you can make better decisions naturally.

Our Philosophy

Healthy habits don’t come from pressure. They come from clarity. No complex logging. No guilt. Just understanding.

Important Note

MealMind provides general nutrition information for awareness purposes only. It is not medical advice and should not replace professional healthcare guidance.

If you have a medical condition or dietary restriction, consult a qualified professional.

Privacy

Your food logs belong to you. We do not sell personal nutrition data. Your entries are used only to generate insights inside the app.
